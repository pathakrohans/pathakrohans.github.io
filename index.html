About Me

Hello! I'm Rohan, a second-year Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Cornell focused on building dependable systems that blend custom hardware with thoughtful software. I enjoy moving quickly from napkin sketches to working prototypes and refining them until they can withstand real-world constraints.

Recent coursework and projects have revolved around:

  • Digital design and verification with Verilog/VHDL, FPGA bring-up, and hardware-in-the-loop testing.
  • Embedded Linux and bare-metal development for STM32 and Raspberry Pi platforms.
  • Data acquisition circuits, PCB layout, and mixed-signal debug tooling.

I'm currently seeking 2025 internship opportunities where I can ship robust embedded platforms, collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, and continue learning how to turn great ideas into production-quality hardware/software systems.
